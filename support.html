Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us

Contact

Email brainthinkersstudio@proton.me. Please include your phone model and the app version (shown in Settings › About). We usually reply within a few days.

Ads and privacy

How do I change my ad consent choices?

Open Settings › Privacy options in the app. This option appears in regions where consent is required (for example the EU, EEA and UK).

Can I turn ads off?

No. The game is free and funded by ads. Rewarded videos, which give coins, a heart or extra moves, are always optional. Other ads can be limited by declining personalised ads in Settings › Privacy options, where that option is shown.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the game stores is on your phone: Settings › Reset all progress clears your game, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data. We don't run servers or accounts, so there's no copy with us. For data Google holds for ads, see the privacy policy.

Notifications

How do I stop reminders?

Turn off Settings › Notifications in the game, or block notifications for Tidepool in Android's settings. Reminders never arrive at night.
